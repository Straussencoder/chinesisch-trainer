# Prompts für die 17 Etappen-Bilder

So geht's:
1. In ChatGPT (oder einem anderen Bildgenerator) einen neuen Chat öffnen.
2. Für jedes Bild: zuerst den **Stil-Block** und darunter den Prompt der Etappe einfügen. Wenn alles in einem Chat läuft, kannst du nach dem ersten Bild auch schreiben: „Gleicher Stil wie eben, jetzt: …“ und nur den Etappen-Prompt anhängen.
3. Format: Querformat **3:2** (z. B. 1536 × 1024).
4. Prüf jedes Bild kurz: **keine Schrift, keine Zeichen, keine Schilder, kein Stempel**. Bildgeneratoren malen sonst Fantasie-Zeichen hinein. Wenn doch, einfach neu erzeugen lassen.
5. Die Bilder als PNG oder JPG hochladen, am besten mit der Nummer im Dateinamen (01.png …). Einbau: siehe CLAUDE.md, Abschnitt „Etappen-Bilder“.

## Stil-Block (immer mitschicken)

```
Traditional Chinese ink wash painting (shuimo) on warm, slightly textured rice paper. Soft grey and black ink washes, confident brush strokes, wet edges and dry-brush texture, lots of empty paper. Muted palette: ink black, warm greys, a pale wash of jade green or ochre where it helps, and one small accent of vermilion red. Calm, poetic, hand-painted look, like a page from a travel sketchbook by a skilled painter. Landscape format 3:2. Main subject in the middle and upper part of the picture; keep the bottom quarter quiet and light (paper, mist or water) because text will be placed over it. Small human figures only, seen from a distance, no faces in close-up. Absolutely no text, no Chinese characters, no letters, no signs with writing, no red seal stamps, no signature, no frame, no border.
```

## Teil 1: Peking → Shanghai

**01 北京 · Ankunft in Peking**
```
Arrival in Beijing: the vermilion Tiananmen-style gate tower with its double roof seen from a distance across a wide square at dawn, a few tiny travellers with a suitcase in the foreground, soft morning mist, swallows in the sky.
```

**02 胡同 · In den Hutongs**
```
A narrow Beijing hutong alley: grey brick walls, a red wooden courtyard door with round door knockers, an old bicycle leaning on the wall, a persimmon tree hanging over the wall, two neighbours chatting in the distance.
```

**03 长城 · An der Großen Mauer**
```
The Great Wall at Mutianyu winding over misty mountain ridges in autumn, several watchtowers along the ridge getting smaller into the distance, layered mountains fading into fog, a single tiny hiker on the wall.
```

**04 茶馆 · Im Teehaus**
```
Inside a quiet traditional Beijing teahouse: a wooden table by a lattice window, a clay teapot and small cups with rising steam, a bowl of rice and a plate of vegetables, soft light through the window, a hanging bird cage in the background.
```

**05 市场 · Auf dem Markt**
```
A lively evening street market in China under rows of red paper lanterns: stalls with baskets of fruit and vegetables, a vendor with a wide straw hat, warm lantern glow, tiny shoppers in the distance.
```

**06 高铁 · Mit dem Schnellzug**
```
A sleek white high-speed train speeding through a wide green landscape of rice fields and low hills between Beijing and Shanghai, motion suggested by long horizontal brush strokes, a distant village and a pagoda on a hill.
```

**07 外滩 · Am Bund in Shanghai**
```
The Bund in Shanghai: the row of old European-style stone buildings along the river with the clock tower of the Custom House, the Huangpu river in front with a small boat, soft evening light, the skyline of modern towers only faintly in the background mist.
```

**08 水乡 · Im Wasserdorf**
```
A water town near Shanghai like Zhouzhuang: a calm canal with a stone arch bridge, whitewashed houses with black tiled roofs, a wooden boat with a boatwoman, willow branches, reflections in the water.
```

## Teil 2: Ein Monat in Shanghai

**09 弄堂 · In einer Gasse in Shanghai**
```
A Shanghai shikumen lane (longtang): two-storey brick houses with carved stone gate frames, laundry on bamboo poles between the windows, potted plants, an old grandmother sitting on a small stool, a child playing.
```

**10 学校 · In der Sprachschule**
```
A bright classroom of a language school: wooden desks, a large blackboard that is completely blank, open notebooks and brushes on a desk, a window with plane tree leaves outside, a few students seen from behind.
```

**11 南京路 · Auf der Nanjing-Straße**
```
Nanjing Road in Shanghai at night: a long pedestrian shopping street with glowing shop windows and lights painted as soft colour washes, crowds of tiny shoppers, an old tram in the middle of the street. Shop signs only as blurred blocks of light, no readable writing.
```

**12 豫园 · Im Yu-Garten**
```
The Yu Garden in Shanghai: a zigzag stone bridge over a lotus pond, a pavilion with upturned roof corners, rough scholar rocks, a cat sitting on a stone at the edge of the pond.
```

**13 西湖 · Am Westsee in Hangzhou**
```
West Lake in Hangzhou in light drizzle: weeping willows on the shore, a small wooden boat on the calm water, an arched causeway bridge, misty hills with a pagoda in the distance, rain suggested by fine grey washes.
```

**14 饭店 · Im Restaurant**
```
A family dinner in a Chinese restaurant: a round table with a lazy Susan full of shared dishes, bowls of rice, chopsticks, a teapot, a plate of fruit, warm light from a hanging lamp, the family only suggested at the edges of the table.
```

**15 东方明珠 · Am Fernsehturm**
```
The Oriental Pearl TV Tower in Shanghai at dusk, seen from across the Huangpu river, its spheres glowing softly, a few modern towers faintly behind it, reflections on the water, a ferry crossing.
```

**16 人民公园 · Im Volkspark**
```
People's Park in Shanghai in the morning: a group of older people doing tai chi under big trees, a grandfather on a bench with a small dog, a path with a lamp post, soft morning mist.
```

**17 机场 · Am Flughafen Pudong**
```
Pudong airport at sunrise seen from outside: a large modern terminal with a curved roof, an airplane taking off into a pale sky, a lone traveller with a backpack watching from the foreground, a feeling of farewell.
```
